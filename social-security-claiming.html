    Enter amounts in today’s dollars, as your Social Security statement shows them. With COLA on, every check rises by the yearly COLA each January after this year, including the years before you claim, the way Social Security applies it. With COLA off, amounts stay exactly as entered. Figures are before taxes and Medicare premiums. Ages are counted from the birth month. Investing assumes every check is invested the month it arrives, nothing is withdrawn, and it compounds monthly at a steady yearly return with no taxes or fees. It’s a what-if, not a forecast. The Example person uses a full-retirement benefit of $2,100, close to the U.S. average for retired workers in 2026, with the standard reductions for claiming at 62 (70%) and credits for waiting to 70 (124%).
